DEL Terminal Edge Cases and Exceptions.
If a passenger arrives with a pet, they must have prior approval from the airline and the Airport Health Organization (APHO) clearance.
If a 150ml medicine bottle is flagged during x-ray, the security personnel must manually inspect it using ETD (Explosive Trace Detector). Once cleared, the passenger can proceed.
Unattended baggage will be cordoned off and reported to the CISF bomb disposal squad immediately.
Flight delays exceeding 4 hours require the airline to provide meals to passengers at designated food courts.
