Delhi International Airport (DEL) Standard Operating Procedure (SOP).
Security checks for passengers:
Passengers are allowed to carry liquid items in containers of up to 100ml. However, essential medicines and baby food can be carried in larger quantities, including 150ml or 200ml bottles, provided they are declared at the security checkpoint and undergo additional screening.
Passengers must present a valid boarding pass and government-issued photo ID to enter the terminal.
Baggage drop counters close exactly 60 minutes prior to the scheduled departure time for domestic flights, and 75 minutes for international flights.
Duty-free allowances depend on the destination country, but a standard limit is 2 liters of alcohol per passport.
